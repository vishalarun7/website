Impact

I put the impact I'll have during my life on a spectrum. On one end are the vast direct impacts of my vocation (working on something useful to society, creating jobs, improving lives etc.) On the other end is the butterfly effect of the secondary and tertiary impacts - the people I help, inspire, learn from etc.

I'm in the search of a position that would let me maximise my impact on both ends of that spectrum. Somewhere where I can use my ability to break down and solve complex problems, my agency and ability to convince people of things, and contribute to and reap the benefits of being in great company. If that sounds like the kind of person you'd like to work with, I would love to hear from you.
